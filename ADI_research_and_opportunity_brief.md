## 2. Strategic moves that create consulting demand

1. **AI data center is the new growth engine.** ADI is positioned as a "grid-to-core" power and optical supplier, including 800V-to-intermediate-bus conversion (20 kW, >2.5 kW/in³).
   - Empower Semiconductor: $1.5B, closed 7 Jul 2026. Integrated voltage regulators.
   - Alif Semiconductor: $1.35B upfront plus up to $200M contingent, announced 9 Sep 2026, expected to close in Q4 CY2026. Edge-AI microcontrollers.
   - Maxim: revenue synergies contributed "hundreds of millions" in FY25.
   - That makes three integrations in roughly four years, and the last two are fabless or early-scale companies being absorbed into a large manufacturer.
2. **Hybrid manufacturing.** ADI has 10 internal fabs plus external foundries such as TSMC. It targets "swing" capacity across internal and external sites for about 70% of revenue.
   - $2.7B invested in capex since Maxim. FY26 capex is expected at 4–6% of revenue. Q3 capex was $0.6B.
   - Major projects: Beaverton OR ($1B+, 8-inch conversion), Limerick (€630M), Cavite Philippines assembly/test.
   - Penang assembly/test sold to ASE (completed May 2026) under a long-term supply agreement. This is a deliberate shift of assembly/test toward an outsourced model.
3. **Supply tightness and strategic inventory.**
   - Utilization is already high. ADI is adding tools in existing space and buying more external wafers.
   - It built a strategic "die bank": inventory +$83M sequentially to a record level, with days of inventory at 156.
   - Channel inventory is below the 6–7 week target.
   - Industry lead times are starting to extend.
   - Q4 pricing adjustments are part of the margin guide.
4. **Channel.** About 55% of sales go through distribution. Arrow is the global volume distributor (~$2B of Arrow's revenue historically). Digi-Key and Mouser are eCommerce partners, and Farnell/Newark/element14 are regional.
5. **Enterprise AI.** The SambaNova Suite was deployed in 2024 for field sales and datasheet knowledge. There is an internal Enterprise AI team hiring for agentic AI application roles.
6. **Policy and geopolitical exposure.** ADI cites export controls, China concentration risk, tariffs and duties in its filings. A 25% tariff on certain advanced semiconductors was proclaimed in Jan 2026. CHIPS Act status is uncertain.
